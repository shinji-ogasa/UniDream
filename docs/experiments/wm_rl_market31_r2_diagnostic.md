## Findings

1. **The imagined market is materially miscalibrated, so the DD comparison has no active contrast.** The frozen reward head's raw 15m log-return outputs on the T probe have mean `+0.0025261296`, SD `0.0000263686`, and 128/128 positive signs. Corresponding T actuals have mean `−0.0001101568`, SD `0.0032976022`, and 65 positive / 63 negative signs. Mean prediction minus target is `+0.0026362864` (26.36 bp per 15m). The 300 saved updates in every arm report zero strategy drawdown, zero benchmark drawdown, and zero DD delta; portfolio simple reward is about +0.0025 per imagined 15m. The DD2.5 and DD5 strong-anchor arms have exactly identical 300 log records, actor tensors and critic tensors. Their penalty coefficient cannot change this run when its nonnegative hinge argument is inactive. This is not evidence that downside control is useless in the real market.

2. **BC equals B&H because its executed changes fall inside the fixed deadband.** The T teacher supplies 34,711/35,830 exact benchmark positions, 1,119 underweights, and zero overweights. BC chunk targets similarly contain 8,654/8,948 exact benchmark positions and 294 underweights. Completed V has 5,031 finite intents among 8,832 scheduled rows; BC intents range `0.9988029003–1.0005739927`. These outputs are not identically one, but all are inside the 0.01 execution deadband from initial benchmark exposure, so the saved trace has zero fills and exactly B&H accounting. No new economics were computed.

3. **The AC teacher replay is a different loss, not simply more of the BC policy's fitting objective.** BC's residual target loss is scaled smooth-L1 and its trade target is the gap divided by an inferred 0.5 scale. AC replay uses a distribution negative log-likelihood plus a binary gap>1e−8 trade target. Both paths already apply imbalance weighting to target losses; it would be wrong to claim that only AC reweights. AC's full original-grid bank has 1,137 trade and 34,693 hold rows, so its trade weight is about 30.51. The stronger anchor therefore strongly reinforces a sparse, solely-underweight teacher under a changed objective. Saved gradient diagnostics measure the weighted BC and AC components: in the strong-anchor arm they are 1.80–9.84 versus 0.015–0.041 at the four diagnostic steps. This supports replay loss dominating those sampled gradients, not a complete causal attribution of every actor update.

4. **The direction of completed physical behavior matches that pressure.** Saved decay trace has two sales, strong-anchor trace three sales, and no rebuy fills. Mean intent is approximately 0.9841 / 0.9781 respectively; stronger-anchor mean exposure is 0.97666. The completed quarter's B&H return is already reported as +105.735%, despite its start-of-quarter regime label being “bear.” Start regime is not the realized whole-quarter trend. Thus persistent underweight reduces Alpha in this observed quarter. The frozen T probe independently shows trade probability rising from BC mean 0.0439 to AC mean 0.3833 / 0.4144 and target standard deviation shrinking from 0.1180 to 0.0224 / 0.0217. These are model-output diagnostics, not newly executed policies. The saved 0.7 threshold comparison is descriptive only; the release evaluator's actual continuous-mode intent and deadband determine fills.

5. **Predictive coverage is not calibration.** Passing all 42 auxiliary target-gradient checks proves active supervision exists. It does not establish useful forecasts. Auxiliary return/risk/utility units use scale 100; crash outputs are logits. The market reward head instead uses unscaled raw 15m returns against the existing 255 symlog bins (adjacent central-bin spacing about 0.1575). That large target/bin scale mismatch is a plausible source of poor small-return fitting, but the current observations do not isolate it from finite training or decoder mass. Do not state that the features lack signal, or that a teacher change alone will solve this.

## Smallest next step if the final fixed matrix does not qualify

Do not add another alpha/DD sweep. First register a **single reward-head unit/calibration correction**, keeping architecture, input features, teacher and one chosen AC schedule fixed: for example, the already-established factor-100 return units at the market head, with mandatory inverse scaling when decoding before portfolio accounting and checkpoint metadata that rejects mixed units. Factor 100 is a fixed existing convention, not an outcome-optimized parameter. Before any new AC economic comparison, verify on a T-internal chronological diagnostic that decoded reward magnitude/sign variation and imagined downside support are no longer degenerate. This is a proposed bounded correction, not a claim it will improve Alpha/DD. A conditional-mean decoder can still omit stochastic market tails after its bias is fixed, so lack of downward imagined paths may persist and must be reported.

The next teacher-focused correction, if separately authorized rather than bundled into that contrast, should align AC replay's structured target/trade semantics with actual BC pretraining (or explicitly treat it as a different weighted teacher objective). Reuse the same causal teacher and original-grid states. Do not claim “stronger BC anchoring” alone preserves the fitted BC policy while those losses differ. No hindsight Oracle should be substituted as if it were a learned causal policy.
